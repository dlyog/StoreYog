Research-only model

The StoreYog small model is our fine-tune of Meta’s OPT-350M, which Meta licenses under the OPT License Agreement for non-commercial research use only.

It is offered on this page only for the hackathon demonstration and our research comparison of tool-calling approaches. It is not used, and will never be used, in any commercial product or service of DLYog Lab Research Services LLC. Other modes use separately licensed models.

Store data here is synthetic. Requests in every mode pass the same responsible-AI guard and are recorded in the store audit.

Simulator settings

Continuous duplex voice keeps the microphone open, detects the end of your turn, and lets you interrupt a spoken reply. Pause briefly to send your question, and say “stop” to turn off the microphone.

Background music: “North of Midnight”, an AI-generated track (Google Gemini).

Modes compare tool-calling approaches on the same store: Gemma router (Gemma picks one action from examples; code runs the tools), StoreYog small model (our 350M fine-tune), and Gemma native tools. Every request passes the same responsible-AI guard.

The demo script uses a scripted assistant and real MCP calls. Typed and spoken requests use the configured local model, or Claude when an Anthropic key is present. No real purchases are made.
